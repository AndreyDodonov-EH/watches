Subject: "DAYFALL — DISK BRACELET", a wristwatch built as a bracelet of eleven IDENTICAL round disks — all 28 mm diameter, 5 mm thick, matte brushed titanium, thin polished chamfer — linked edge-to-edge by hinges. From outside the disks are indistinguishable; the electronics are DISTRIBUTED inside them, one function per disk, and connected disk-to-disk through the hinges. The display disk is the same size as every other disk; only its interior differs.
